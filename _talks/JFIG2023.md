At J.FIG 2023 in Montpellier, I presented the prelude of my work on generating coral reef islands through an iterative terrain generation process. The work was co-authored with Loïc Barthe, Bedrich Benes, Oliver Deussen, and Noura Faraj.

This work is still on its way and will be shortly published.
