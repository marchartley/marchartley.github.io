*Génération procédurale d’environnements 3D sous-marins*

I defended this PhD thesis in computer science at the University of Montpellier on 15 December 2025. I carried out the research at LIRMM under the supervision of Christophe Fiorio, Noura Faraj, and Karen Godary-Dejean.

Testing underwater robots at sea is costly, and mission conditions can be difficult to reproduce. I developed interactive tools for building virtual test environments. The methods cover generating coral reef islands from sketches with cGANs, placing coral colonies according to local environmental conditions using Environmental Objects, and simulating terrain erosion with particle systems.
