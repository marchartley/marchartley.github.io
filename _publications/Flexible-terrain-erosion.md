We developed a particle-based erosion method that works with height fields, voxel grids, material layers, and implicit surfaces. The method models material removal, transport, and deposition separately from the fluid simulation. A fluid simulation can be given as an external velocity field guiding individual particles, which lets users control how erosion develops across the terrain.
