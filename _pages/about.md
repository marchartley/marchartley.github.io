## About me
I work on procedural modeling, terrain generation, and simulation for computer graphics. I defended my PhD in computer science at the University of Montpellier on 15 December 2025. At LIRMM, I worked with the ICAR team and collaborated with the Marine Robotics team on virtual environments for underwater robot testing.

In my [doctoral research]({{ '/publication/Thesis' | relative_url }}), I developed interactive tools for generating coral reef environments and simulating erosion across different terrain representations. I'm interested in how users can steer procedural tools and predict the effects of their edits.

My paper [*Flexible terrain erosion*]({{ '/publication/Flexible-terrain-erosion' | relative_url }}) received the CGI 2024 Best Student Paper Award while [*A low-cost machine learning process for gait measurement using biomechanical sensors*]({{ '/publication/A-low-cost-machine-learning-process-for-gait-measurement-using-biomechanical-sensors.md' | relative_url }}) received the IMEKO 2021 Best Student Paper Award. 

I held a teaching and thesis appointment (MCE) at the University of Montpellier from October 2021 to August 2024, teaching and research appointment (ATER) at Polytech Montpellier from November 2024 to August 2025, and at the University of Montpellier from November 2025 to August 2026.

## Research 

I prioritize predictability to ensure the generated results are interpretable and aligned with user preferences. My approach emphasizes user-in-the-loop procedural generation, where user input drives the algorithm's decisions, moving away from purely automated processes.

My fields of interest are mainly focused around:

- Procedural modeling
- Terrain generation
- Texture generation
- Particle systems
- Animal behaviour
- Creative coding
